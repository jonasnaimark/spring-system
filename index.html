Gestures

  
Gestures enable fluid, physical navigation alongside tap-based controls.

  

    
Grow Dismiss

    
Sheet Dismiss

  

  

    

      

        

          
Swipe direction

          

            

              
Down

              
Grow transitions dismiss when swiping down from the top of a scroll view.

            

            

              
Edge

              
Swiping the left edge also dismisses a fullscreen grow transition.

            

          

        

        

          
Thresholds

          

            

              
Position

              
A swipe beyond 50 points commits navigation on release. Releasing early reverts.

            

            

              
Velocity

              
A swipe faster than 1000 pts/sec commits navigation on release.

            

          

        

        

          
Gesture driven motion

          

            

              
Resistance

              
The further you swipe toward the opposite edge, the less it follows your input.

            

            

              
Scale

              
Elements scale down as they're dismissed.

            

            

              
Scrim

              
The scrim fades out during dismiss swipes.

Accessibility

  
Some people experience motion as disorienting or distracting. These platform settings reduce or eliminate animation.

  

    

      

        

          
Reduced motion

          

            

              
iOS & macOS

              
Transitions that scale, slide, or use parallax are replaced with simple 150ms crossfades.

            

            

              
Windows & Android

              
All animation and motion is disabled.

            

          

        

        

          
Animated assets

          

            

              
Icons & illustrations

              
Animated illustrations and icons must have a static fallback.

            

            

              
Video

              
Auto-playing video must be paused.
