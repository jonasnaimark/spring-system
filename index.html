Thresholds
Position

A swipe beyond 100 points commits navigation on release. Releasing early reverts. Haptics confirms the threshold.

Velocity

A swipe faster than 1000 pts/sec commits navigation on release.

Accessibility

Some people find motion disorienting or distracting. Respect these platform settings:

Reduced motion
iOS & macOS

Dramatic transitions that scale, slide, or use parallax are replaced with simple 150ms fades.

Windows & Android

All animation and motion is turned off.

Animated assets
Icons & illustrations

Animated illustrations and icons are replaced with static versions.

Video

Auto-playing videos are paused.

Default motion
Reduced motion
